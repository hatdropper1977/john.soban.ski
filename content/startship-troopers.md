Summary: A sold lot record of Starship Troopers props indicates that both Johnny Rico and Carmen Ibanez wore wrist watches in Paul Verhoeven's 1997 TriStar Pictures feature Starship Troopers.

For three decades movie watch obsessives believed that Casper Van Dien did not wear a wristwatch in Paul Verhoeven's 1997 TriStar Pictures feature **Starship Troopers**.  In August 2026, for example, I asked Gemini what watch he wore and it replied:

> Johnny Rico doesn't actually wear a notable or identifiable wristwatch in the film.
> 
> When you see the word "watch" associated with his character in search results or movie summaries, it refers to his duty shift, not a piece of jewelry.

Today I will prove Gemini wrong.

![Van Dien fighting a bug]({static}/images/Troopers/00_Rico_Action.jpg)

## The Clue
I have an obsessive affinity for Starship Troopers, and constantly look for props on auction.  In my research, I discovered the June 15th, 2018  **Profiles in History: Icons & Legends of Hollywood** auction, that contained several Starshoop Troopers pieces.

This included a lot of [200 props from both Starship Troopers and the Running Man](https://entertainment.ha.com/itm/movie-tv-memorabilia/starship-troopers-and-the-running-man-massive-collection-of-props-and-production-materials/a/997049-2893.s?ic16=ViewItem-BrowseTabs-Auction-Archive-ThisAuction-120115).

The description mentions two watches from Johnny Rico, and two from Carmen Ibanez.

> **Starship Troopers and The Running Man massive collection of props and production materials.** (TriStar, 1997 & 1987) Large collection of (200+) props and production materials including (12) **Johnny Rico (Casper Van Dien)** props: 8-dog tags, 1-wallet, **2-wrist watches** and 1-handkerchief, (9) "Lt. Carmen Ibanez" (Denise Richards) props: 4-dog tags, 2-wrist watches and 3-FED photo I.D. cards, (1) referee's whistle, (1) spider's leg pendant on a neck chain, (3) "Corporal Birdie" (Ungela Brockman) props: 2-grenades in a bloody holder, 1-range finder, (80+) production Polaroids of cast and FX, (20) photo I.D. cards, (2) clean grenades in a pouch, (1) field monitor in web pouch and from The Running Man (2) futuristic aluminum handcuffs and (75+) "ICS stickers, decals, embroidered patches and pins. All in production used good to fine condition.

In the picture, we see two wrist watches, one masculine (lower picture) and one feminine (upper picture):

![Props lot from 2018]({static}/images/Troopers/01_Ha_Props.jpg)

We can dismiss any theory that the watch belongs to Ben Richards from the **Running Man**, because Arnold Schwarzenegger wore his eponymous **Seiko Arnie** in that picture.  By simple deduction, the watch **must** belong to Johnny Rico.

## The Investigation
I clipped and rotated Rico's watch from the HA picture above.

![Rotated Watch Pic]({static}/images/Troopers/02_Rico_Rotate.jpg)

We see the text **Avocet** on the face of the watch.

A simple Google search confirms that this matches the Avocet Street.

## Evidence

- Cinematic Widescreen LCD Screen
- Rubber Select Button
- Barbell Protrusions on Case
- Strap Width and Shape
- Half Moon Lower half with Strip on Bottom
- Location of small white writing and indicators on watch 

Avocent released this watch in 1993, and four years of popularity strengthens the argument that the Prop Master could easily purchase the watch at retail.

TriStar hired William Peretta in the role of Propmaster, and he worked out of Sony's Astaire Building in Culver City, CA.  William Peretta also held membership in the Hollywood Local 44, the Hollywood Craft Local.  Avocet ran operations out of Silicone Valley.  While not conclusive, we can surmise that Mr. Peretta could source a California watch brand in Los Angeles with ease.

You may ask, "would a Propmaster in a big budget, Hollywood blockbuster source a wristwatch from a Mall?"  I answer **yes**, with certainty.  Art director Roger Christian, for example, bought a **futuristic, crazy looking watch** for Ridley Scott's 1979 20th Century Fox Feature **Alien**.  Ripley wears a Casio A100.

![Ripley wears a Casio in Alien]({static}/images/Troopers/03_Ripley_Casio.jpg)

## Carmen's Watch
Troopers' Space Pompadoured Heart-breaker Carmen Ibanez also wore a watch.

![Space Pompadour]({static}/images/Troopers/07_Carmen_Pic.jpg)

I clipped and rotated her watch.

![Rotate Carmen's Watch]({static}/images/Troopers/08_Carmen_Rotate.jpg)

Carmen owns a steel watch with fat case, thin bracelet, and a simple face with no numerals.

This investigation proves more difficult.  Again, we want an easy to source watch, available in the mid 90's, available to purchase in Los Angeles for a reasonable price.

If you think of LA Malls in the 90s, you will think of Skagen.

One Skagen Option includes the following.

![A Skagen Watch]({static}/images/Troopers/09_Skagen_Watch.jpg)

The case however, seems too square.

STORM London checks the boxes, Rectangle Cases, Blank Faces, 90s pedigree.

![A Storm Watch]({static}/images/Troopers/10_Storm_Watch.jpg)

This example, however, seem a bit off, with its thin case.

Perhaps the low resolution of the picture hides the details.  If we give the search some leeway, the Esprit fits the bill.

![An Esprit Watch]({static}/images/Troopers/11_Esprit_Watch.jpg)

Malls sold these watches at an affordable price, so it hits Rico's range.  Also, Esprit feels very **High School Girl**.

If you have any luck finding the model, please let me know!
